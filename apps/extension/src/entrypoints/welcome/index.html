Welcome to LoadEarn

While ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek is writing a reply, LoadEarn shows one small sponsored ad below your message. You earn money for the ads you see.

What LoadEarn reads

Only whether the AI is generating and where your last message sits on the page. Never the text of your chats.

What LoadEarn sends

The site the ad appeared on, which ad it was, and when it was seen or clicked. This goes to LoadEarn's servers. When an ad comes from an advertising partner, your browser also lets that partner know it was shown or clicked, so they receive your IP address and browser type, as with ads anywhere on the web. No chat content and no browsing history.
